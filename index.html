About

Olivia Dal Pra is a writer and filmmaker from Northeast Ohio. She studied Digital Media Production at Kent State University, during which she won multiple university honors and scholarships for projects she wrote and directed. She spent a number of years as a writer, cast member, and eventually assistant producer of Kent’s student-led sketch comedy shows The Agenda and Grab Bag. After graduation, she worked various positions before moving home to take care of her grandmother with Alzheimer's, during which she continued to write, produce comedy film projects, and volunteer with the Cleveland Comedy Festival. When not working or writing, Olivia loves watching hockey, playing video games, and chilling with her furry feline friends.
