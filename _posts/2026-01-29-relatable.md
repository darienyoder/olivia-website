What’s the most #relatable thing you’ve done?
I try to get in at least one per day.
My favorite in recent memory was last week
some guy cut me off in traffic and I fantasized
about him careening off the highway and crashing
into a Jared’s billboard.
Judging by the beater he was driving
he likely couldn’t afford jewelry from there
and it would add insult to injury 
as he flew impossibly towards his demise
in the bullseye center of a diamond necklace.
