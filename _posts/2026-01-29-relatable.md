What’s the most #relatable thing you’ve done?<br>
I try to get in at least one per day.<br>
My favorite in recent memory was last week<br>
some guy cut me off in traffic and I fantasized<br>
about him careening off the highway and crashing<br>
into a Jared’s billboard.<br>

and it would add insult to injury<br>
as he flew impossibly towards his demise<br>
in the bullseye center of a diamond necklace.
