title:  "Ohio Passes Law Drawing New District Around Every Individual Democrat"
---
COLUMBUS, OH - In order to ensure the best representation in this year’s midterm elections, Ohio passed a new congressional map creating a single district drawn around every individual Democrat. “Our revolutionary map outlines that each Democrat in the state will be part of one big district with one representative, with the borders constantly following them wherever they go.” said a spokesperson for the state legislature. “Using location tracking, we are able to determine where each Democrat is and update our congressional map on a minute-by-minute basis. It’s the best way to keep things fluid and fair to those of us who intend to cast our votes correctly this November.” The issue was initially challenged by Democratic members of the legislature and made it all the way to the U.S. Supreme Court, where it was allowed to stand in a 6-1-1-1 decision. The technology is also said to be useful for keeping tabs on Democrats who partake in Republican-Only activities, like campaigning, protesting, and exercising freedom of speech, which Ohio intends to crack down on in the near future. As for the upcoming primary election, every Democrat in the state is to report to a single polling place in a shack on the outskirts of Dayton, with a lone ballot box that looks suspiciously like a shredder.
