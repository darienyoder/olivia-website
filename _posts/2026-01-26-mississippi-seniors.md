title:  "Mississippi High School Seniors Nervous For Shapes And Colors Exam"
---
BILOXI, MS - As summer break for students across the country approaches, high school seniors in the state of Mississippi are frantically studying for their shapes and colors exam. “I always confuse the similar aspects of the color red and shape of a square,” said Jake Flanagan, a student at Southview high school using flashcards to remember the differences between shapes and colors, as well as the names of each one, “I’m not too worried about it though, since I’ll probably just pass automatically like I’ve been doing since fourth grade.” Jake and other students at Southview are also preparing for a variety of other exams, like Menu Comprehension, Googling, and Clock. “We pride ourselves in offering our students education that translates into real-world skills.” said Superintendent Michael Toews, “When you look outside your window, you don’t see books. You see other things like green, triangles, and Taco Bell. We hope our students benefit from this curriculum and succeed in all aspects of their lives.” Jake will be a Pre-Med student at the University of Mississippi this upcoming fall.
